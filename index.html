Pipe for Excel

Demo add-in that writes audit working papers into your workbook: materiality, risk, journals, sampling, detail testing and misstatements. All AI output is simulated.

Install in Excel on the web

  1. Download manifest.xml.
  2. Open a blank workbook at excel.office.com.
  3. Go to Home › Add-ins › More add-ins › My add-ins › Upload my add-in and choose the manifest.
  4. Click Open Pipe on the Home tab.

What it writes

IndexEngagement settings and the working-paper register. Every header links here.
A1Materiality from the TB with SUMIFS, basis selection, PM and clearly trivial as formulas.
A2Revenue analytics and a risk register. Ratings are formulas on likelihood and magnitude.
A3Manual journals scored against six criteria, five of them formulas.
B1Account mapping, sampling plan checked against firm minimums, and the selected items.
D3.1–J1.1One tests-of-detail paper per area with results, known and projected misstatement.
S1Summary of misstatements linked to each paper and compared with materiality.

Using it

  1. Set up the engagement and click Create sample GL & TB.
  2. Audit runs the three workflows in order with a plan to approve and a sign-off for each.
  3. Processes runs any single paper. Pipe checks the workbook for what it needs and asks for the rest.
  4. Shaded cells are judgements. Change them and the papers recalculate.
